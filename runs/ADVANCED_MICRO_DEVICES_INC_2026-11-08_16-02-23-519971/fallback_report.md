## 1. Overview

AMD sells products for data center, client, gaming and embedded markets. In the quarter ended September 26, 2026, GAAP net revenue was $13.377 billion, compared with $9.246 billion in the quarter ended September 27, 2025. The source is the 10-Q, “Condensed Consolidated Statements of Operations,” row “Net revenue”; the earnings release repeats the values in its GAAP quarterly results table. Revenue growth was ($13.377 billion − $9.246 billion) ÷ $9.246 billion = 44.7% (same source and periods).

GAAP diluted earnings per share was $1.46 for the quarter ended September 26, 2026, compared with $0.75 for the quarter ended September 27, 2025 (10-Q, “Condensed Consolidated Statements of Operations,” row “Diluted earnings per share”; earnings release, GAAP quarterly results table). The release’s first paragraph reports rounded quarterly revenue of $13.4 billion, GAAP gross margin of 54%, GAAP operating income of $2.8 billion, GAAP net income of $2.4 billion, and diluted earnings per share of $1.46. The detailed statements give the precise dollar values used below.

## 4. Future Developments and Strategic Recommendations

The near-term catalyst is the Q4 2026 outlook: revenue of $15.0 billion, plus or minus $0.3 billion, with non-GAAP gross margin of about 57% (Exhibit 99.1, “Fourth Quarter 2026 Outlook”). This outlook follows GAAP Q3 revenue of $13,377 million and GAAP gross margin of $7,210 million ÷ $13,377 million = 53.9% for the quarter ended September 26, 2026 (10-Q, “Condensed Consolidated Statements of Operations”). GAAP and non-GAAP margin definitions differ; do not compare them as if they were identical.

Recommendation: track whether Data Center growth converts to operating cash and whether client and gaming weakness stabilizes. The evidence supports both points: Data Center revenue grew 97%, while Client and Gaming revenue fell 8% (Exhibit 99.1, “Segment Summary”). The 10-Q warns that customer deployment delays, data center capacity, power, water, memory supply and other bottlenecks can delay adoption (Part II, Item 1A, risk factors). A material change to this view would be a later filing showing that the main reported growth engine has stopped growing or turned negative; the current Research Analyst persona specifies this condition.

Insight: AMD reported a $1,179 million non-cash impact from customer warrants for the nine months ended September 26, 2026, and its 10-Q says the quarterly warrant liability remeasurement is driven by AMD’s stock price and other valuation assumptions. This can make GAAP earnings more volatile; it is not stated in the release’s first paragraph. Source: 10-Q, “Condensed Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows,” row “Non-cash impact of warrants issued to customers,” and Exhibit 99.1, reconciliation footnote (3): “Change in fair value of warrant liability relating to the quarterly remeasurement of warrants issued to certain customers is driven by changes in AMD’s stock price and other valuation assumptions.”
