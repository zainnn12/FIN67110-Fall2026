## 1. Overview

*Role applied: Research Analyst for the business evidence; Financial Analyst for the statement evidence and arithmetic.*

Costco operates membership warehouses and e-commerce sites. Its model uses “low prices on a limited selection” to seek high sales volume and rapid inventory turnover, together with volume purchasing, efficient distribution, reduced merchandise handling, and no-frills facilities. Costco states that this combination permits it to operate at lower gross margins than most retailers. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, lines 721 and 724-731; tag: not applicable to Business-section text; period: FY2026 and at 2026-08-30 where stated.]

At August 30, 2026, Costco operated **939 warehouses**, compared with **914** at August 31, 2025, and it operated e-commerce sites in **nine named countries**. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 721; tag: not applicable to Business-section operating data; periods: 2026-08-30 and 2025-08-31.] At August 30, 2026, Costco reported **84,100 thousand paid members = 84.1 million** and **150,400 thousand total cardholders = 150.4 million**. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, lines 761-765; tag: not applicable to the membership table; period: 2026-08-30.] Costco reported member renewal rates of **92.3% in the U.S. and Canada** and **89.8% worldwide** at the end of FY2026; these are trailing rates that exclude memberships with expiration dates in the six months before the reporting date. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 770; tag: not applicable to the membership disclosure; period: trailing calculation measured at 2026-08-30.] The filing does not provide the renewal numerator and denominator, so no renewal-rate arithmetic is supplied or inferred.

## 2. Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

*Role applied: Research Analyst, with the Strategy Manager checking that each opportunity and risk remains tied to filing evidence.*

Costco describes its industry as highly competitive on price, merchandise quality and selection, location, convenience, distribution strategy, and customer service. It identifies Walmart, Target, Kroger, and Amazon as significant U.S. general-merchandise competitors and Sam's Club and BJ's Wholesale Club as warehouse-club competitors. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, lines 792-793; tag: not applicable to Competition-section text; period: FY2026 filing disclosure.]

Three filing-supported features define Costco's market position:

1. **Member loyalty.** The reported renewal rates were **92.3% in the U.S. and Canada** and **89.8% worldwide** at the end of FY2026. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 770; tag: not applicable; period: trailing calculation measured at 2026-08-30.] The filing does not supply the numerator and denominator for these reported rates.
2. **Private-label differentiation.** Costco states that Kirkland Signature products are generally priced below national brands, differentiate its merchandise, and generally earn higher margins. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 795; tag: not applicable to Intellectual Property-section text; period: FY2026 filing disclosure.]
3. **Operating efficiency.** Costco attributes efficiency to volume purchasing, cross-docking, reduced handling, pallet displays, limited stock-keeping units, and no-frills facilities. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, lines 724-731; tag: not applicable to Business-section text; period: FY2026 filing disclosure.]

The FY2026 growth evidence is broad but contains conditions. Net sales increased by **$27,335 million**; Costco attributed **$22,457 million** of the increase to comparable sales and the remainder to sales from **25 net new warehouses** opened since the end of FY2025. The filing also reports a **5%** increase in average ticket and a **3%** increase in shopping frequency. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 1074; tag: not applicable to the MD&A disaggregation; period: FY2026 compared with FY2025.] These percentages are company-reported operating measures. The filing does not provide arithmetic inputs for them, so this report does not recalculate them. Digitally enabled comparable sales increased by a reported **21%** in FY2026, both as reported and excluding foreign-currency changes. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 1075; tag: not applicable to the MD&A operating measure; period: FY2026 compared with FY2025.] The filing does not supply the digital-sales numerator and denominator.

Membership-fee revenue was **$5,907 million** in FY2026, compared with **$5,323 million** in FY2025. Management attributes the increase to new member sign-ups, membership-fee increases, and upgrades to Executive memberships. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, lines 1079-1085; tag: not available in the supplied `facts.json`; periods: FY2026 and FY2025.] Because the supplied facts do not include a membership-fee XBRL fact, the line and periods, rather than an unsupported tag, identify this evidence.

The latest saved earnings release also shows higher fourth-quarter revenue and EPS. For the **16 weeks ended August 30, 2026**, total revenue was **$95,723 million** and diluted EPS was **$6.75**. For the **16 weeks ended August 31, 2025**, total revenue was **$86,156 million** and diluted EPS was **$5.87**. [Earnings release, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1, accession 0000909832-26-000084, lines 34-39 and 50-52; tag: not applicable because the supplied release is not the saved XBRL fact source; periods: 16 weeks ended 2026-08-30 and 16 weeks ended 2025-08-31.] The FY2026 quarter included a nonrecurring **$0.15 per diluted share** benefit from IEEPA tariff refunds, net of partial reinvestment in member value. [Earnings release, accession 0000909832-26-000084, line 20; tag: not applicable; period: 16 weeks ended 2026-08-30.] Therefore, the full EPS difference should not be treated as recurring.

Growth remains conditional on execution. Costco states that new-site growth can be affected by property availability, land-use and environmental rules, utility access, construction and operating costs, and competition for suitable locations. It also warns that new warehouses can draw members from existing warehouses and that new-market warehouses and e-commerce sites might not be profitable promptly, or at all. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, lines 825-828; tag: not applicable to Risk Factors; period: FY2026 filing disclosure.] Costco also states that membership loyalty and growth are essential and that damage to its brand, reputation, or culture could reduce comparable sales, renewal rates, net sales, and membership-fee revenue. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, lines 832-834; tag: not applicable to Risk Factors; period: FY2026 filing disclosure.]

## 4. Future Developments and Strategic Recommendations

*Roles applied: Strategy Manager for forward priorities and consistency; Research Analyst and Financial Analyst for the evidence.*

Management plans to open up to **33 new warehouses in FY2027**, including **five relocations**, after opening **28** in FY2026, including **three relocations**. Management also states that it currently intends to spend approximately **$7,500 million** on FY2027 capital expenditures and expects to fund those expenditures with operating cash flow, cash and cash equivalents, and short-term investments. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, lines 1142-1156; tag: not applicable to MD&A plans; periods: FY2026 actual and FY2027 plan.] Management states that the warehouse and capital plans are subject to change and gives no assurance that current expectations will be realized. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 1156; tag: not applicable; period: FY2027 plan as of the FY2026 filing.]

The Strategy Manager recommends the following actions, subject to those filing conditions:

- **Use disciplined site gates for expansion.** Evaluate each planned site for suitable-property access, land-use restrictions, utility constraints, construction and operating costs, local competition, and cannibalization before committing capital. This follows Costco's stated expansion risks. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, lines 825-828; tag: not applicable to Risk Factors; period: FY2026 filing disclosure.]

- **Protect member value and renewal behavior.** Membership growth, Executive-member penetration, and high renewal rates materially influence profitability, and brand or culture damage can weaken renewal and sales. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, lines 1018 and 832-834; tag: not applicable to MD&A and Risk Factors; period: FY2026 filing disclosure.]

- **Continue digital investment without weakening warehouse economics.** Digitally enabled comparable sales increased by a reported **21%** in FY2026, but the filing does not supply numerator-and-denominator arithmetic for that reported rate. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 1075; tag: not applicable to the MD&A operating measure; period: FY2026 compared with FY2025.] The recommendation is to connect digital convenience to the low-cost warehouse model described in the filing. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, lines 724-731; tag: not applicable to Business-section text; period: FY2026 filing disclosure.]

- **Expand Kirkland Signature selectively and protect quality.** Costco states that Kirkland Signature differentiates the assortment and generally earns higher margins, but it also states that consistent quality, competitive pricing, and availability are essential to member loyalty. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, lines 795 and 832-834; tag: not applicable to Business and Risk Factors text; period: FY2026 filing disclosure.]

- **Exclude the tariff-refund benefit from recurring forecasts.** The fourth quarter included a nonrecurring **$0.15 per diluted share** IEEPA tariff-refund benefit, net of partial reinvestment in member value. [Earnings release, accession 0000909832-26-000084, line 20; tag: not applicable; period: 16 weeks ended 2026-08-30.] Costco also states that it intends to invest the majority of subsequent refunds in additional member value. [10-K accession 0000909832-26-000093, lines 1047-1049; tag: not applicable to the tariff disclosure; period: subsequent to FY2026 as described in the filing.]
